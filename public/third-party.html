Third-party software

WhatsBackUp includes open-source components. Their copyright and license files are included beside each component in the installation's resources/app/node_modules folder.

Video processing

FFmpeg 6.1.1 essentials for Windows is distributed through ffmpeg-static. The executable is licensed under GPL version 3. Its license and build configuration are included as ffmpeg.exe.LICENSE and ffmpeg.exe.README in node_modules/ffmpeg-static.

Desktop runtime

Electron (MIT) includes Chromium and Node.js. Electron's license and Chromium notices are also included in the installation folder.
