Welcome to WhatsBackUp

It keeps a copy of every photo, video and message from your WhatsApp — on this PC, and in a cloud folder if you want one.

Before you start, three things worth knowing:

  • This connects to WhatsApp the same way WhatsApp Web does, but it is not an official WhatsApp app. WhatsApp's terms don't allow unofficial clients, and there is a real if small chance an account gets limited or banned for using one.
  • It saves media from everyone you chat with, and they haven't agreed to that. Keep the copies to yourself.
  • Everything stays on this computer and your own cloud folder. There is no account, no server of ours, and no telemetry. The one exception is switched off: AI sorting can send photos and message text to an AI provider you choose, and it will not send anything until you turn it on and accept a second notice that shows you the exact data first.

Bring across your existing copies?

An earlier version of this app was found on this PC. Its media, messages and WhatsApp link can be imported so you don't start over — or scan again.

Where should your media be saved?

Photos and videos can add up to a lot of space, so pick somewhere with room.

Also copy to a cloud folder?

If you use pCloud, OneDrive, Dropbox or a network drive, point this at it and videos are written straight there — so your phone's storage never becomes the only copy.

Sort with AI

Groups your photos into albums by what's in them, and your chats into projects by what they're about. It needs an AI service — your own account, your own key — and it is the only part of this app that sends anything off this PC.
