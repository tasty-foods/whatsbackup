§ 02 Faster than waiting

Three things that answer themselves

These come up most, and each has a full answer already written.

Windows says it protected your PC
Expected, and explained in full on the download page. The installer is not signed with a paid certificate, so Windows does not recognise the publisher. It says nothing about whether the app is safe.
Will this get my account banned
The honest answer, including the part that is not reassuring, is in the questions. WhatsApp's terms do not allow unofficial clients, and the risk is small but real.
Where your data actually goes
Nowhere, unless you switch on AI sorting — and then only to the provider you chose, with your own key. Exactly what it sends →

Something broken rather than unclear? A GitHub issue is the better place — it stays visible, and the fix arrives attached to it.
