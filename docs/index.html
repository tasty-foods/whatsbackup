03 / Your useful little toolkit

Start simple.
Grow into the rest.

You don’t have to set everything up today.
Saving is a useful first step.

01Save the conversationThe words are useful too.+

Search saved message text alongside media and export readable HTML transcripts. WhatsApp captures photos, videos and messages by default; documents, voice notes, audio and stickers are optional.

02Keep your AI imagesA good idea needs somewhere to live.+

Import uploaded and generated ChatGPT images and generated images from Gemini’s Library. Separate sign-ins, source filters and import summaries keep the origins clear.

03Bring a little orderAlbums and projects that you can correct.+

Optional AI labels images and groups content into albums and projects. Search labels and recognised text. Use your own provider key, review the data notice and estimate, and set a monthly budget. Your corrections persist.

04Check your media copiesA clear answer to “did it copy?”+

Verify file presence and matching sizes in your chosen destination folder. Missing copies are flagged and checks expire so you can run them again. These checks do not verify full contents or remote cloud upload completion.

05Clear the clutter carefullyA way back when you change your mind.+

Review possible duplicates and small images before moving files to reversible quarantine. Duplicate candidates use a partial fingerprint, so review matters. Permanent deletion is separate.

06Plan tomorrow’s statusOne less last-minute task.+

Status Studio combines a posting queue, templates, watched folders, albums and optional AI writing. Start in dry run, preview the plan, then decide whether to enable live posting with quiet hours and limits.

Before you begin

A few good questions.

Know what you’re installing and where your files go.

Visit the help centre →
What does WhatsBackUp actually save?

WhatsApp photos, videos and message text, with optional documents, voice notes, audio and stickers. You can also import uploaded and generated images from ChatGPT, and generated images from Gemini’s Library. Each source is linked separately.

Is it free? Do I need an AI subscription?

The desktop app is free. You do not need an AI subscription to save or browse your archive. Optional AI sorting uses your own provider API key; provider charges and limits apply. Review the estimate and set a monthly budget before sorting.

Does it replace WhatsApp’s phone backup?

No. It creates a browsable archive of files and messages on your PC. It does not restore a WhatsApp account or move your chat history to a new phone. Keep your normal phone backup for that.

Can it recover old or deleted pictures?

Import history can collect media that is still available in your linked chats. It cannot recover files that are no longer available from WhatsApp. Skipped and failed items appear in the import summary.

Does my computer need to stay on?

Yes. Capture, imports and scheduled statuses run while the app is running and the required services are reachable. Let it start with Windows if you want it ready in the background.

Where does my data go?

Files and messages are stored on your PC. You choose any cloud folder. Optional AI features send selected content directly to the provider you configure after consent. Source imports connect to their respective services. See the privacy page for the full breakdown.
