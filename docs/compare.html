CHOOSE THE RIGHT TOOL

An archive you can use.
A backup you can restore.

They solve different problems. Keep your phone backup for restoring WhatsApp; use WhatsBackUp for a browsable archive on your PC.

A desktop archive can only keep content it receives or can still import. It cannot recreate unavailable media or restore a WhatsApp account.

THE PRACTICAL CHOICE

Use the tools that fit.

Moving to a new phone?

Use WhatsApp’s supported backup or transfer process. WhatsBackUp does not replace that workflow.

Keeping one conversation?

A manual chat export may be enough if you need a one-off copy and do not need ongoing collection.

Building an archive?

WhatsBackUp keeps a growing collection on your PC, alongside your imported AI images and optional organisation tools.
